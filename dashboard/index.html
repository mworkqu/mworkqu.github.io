How the pieces connect

A client picks parts from his own shelf or buys them from the shop. Buying reserves store stock; delivery moves it. When a product drops below its reorder point the admin makes a batch, a workshop builds it, and marking it ready puts it back on the shelf. The rules are written down in docs/inventory-model.md.
