GESTALTUNG is an engineering studio and manufacturing platform based in Doha, Qatar. We work at the intersection of industrial design, mechanical engineering, and precision fabrication.

Philosophy

The brief is a hypothesis. The object is the answer.

We approach every project as an engineering problem — not an aesthetic exercise. Form is always the result of decisions made under constraints: materials, processes, tolerances, cost, and use. When those decisions are made with rigour, the result tends to look right.

GESTALTUNG was founded to close the gap between the design studio and the factory floor. Too often, products are designed without understanding what they'll require to make, and manufactured without understanding what they were meant to be. We operate across both.

Our studio handles concept development, engineering, and documentation. Our manufacturing network produces the parts. Our shop sells objects that came through the same process. One consistent standard, end to end.

Principles How we think

01

Constraints are the brief.

Every design problem has a set of constraints. Material cost, manufacturing process, assembly sequence, maintenance access, regulatory compliance. We start with the constraints and let the design follow.

02

Documents are deliverables.

A concept that can't be communicated precisely is a concept that can't be built. We hold technical documentation to the same standard as the design itself — clear, complete, and unambiguous.

03

Tolerance is intent made measurable.

A dimension without a tolerance is a wish. We define tolerances based on function, not convention, and we understand the manufacturing implications of every number we write down.

04

One standard, end to end.

Design and manufacturing are not separate disciplines in this studio. The same rigour that goes into the model goes into the drawing, the quote, the first article inspection, and the delivery. No handover gap.
