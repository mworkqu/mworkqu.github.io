About

Built on the belief that
engineering is a discipline.

GESTALTUNG is an engineering studio and manufacturing platform based in Doha, Qatar. We design, fabricate, and deliver precision-made objects.

Philosophy

Form follows function.
Function follows constraint.

We do not decorate. We do not add. Every dimension exists because the part demands it. Every surface finish is specified because the application requires it. We treat design as a sequence of decisions made under constraint — material, process, tolerance, cost, and time.

GESTALTUNG — from the German: form, shape, structure. The act of giving coherent form to a system. That is the only brief we accept.

Principles

01

Precision over speed

We set realistic timelines and hold them. A part delivered late and correct is better than a part delivered wrong on time.

02

Fixed scope, fixed price

Every project is quoted with a clear scope. We do not bill by the hour. Surprises are our problem to absorb, not yours.

03

One point of contact

You deal with the engineer running your project. No account managers, no handoffs, no information lost in translation.

04

Documentation as standard

Every deliverable includes full documentation — drawings, BOMs, material certs, and inspection reports where applicable.
