FAQ

Common questions.

Working with GESTALTUNG — project intake, file formats, lead times, materials, shipping, and payment.

01 · Studio
How does a project start?

Submit a brief through the Studio request form or email studio@gestaltung.qa. A senior engineer reviews every brief and responds within 24 hours, working days, with a structured next step — usually a fixed-scope quotation or a request for clarifying information.

What file formats do you accept?

Native: SolidWorks (SLDPRT / SLDASM), Fusion 360 (F3D), CATIA, Rhino (3DM), AutoCAD (DWG). Neutral: STEP, IGES, STL, OBJ, DXF, PDF. We also work from sketches, point-cloud scans, and physical samples for reverse engineering. If you have something else, ask.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes. Mutual NDAs are standard for any project before file exchange. We can use your template or provide ours. All client files and project data are treated as confidential by default — included in our standard engagement terms.

What does a project cost?

Every project is quoted with a fixed scope and a fixed price — we do not bill by the hour. Studio engagements typically range from 5,000 QAR (single-part DFM reviews) to 50,000+ QAR (full product development). You will receive an itemised quotation before any work begins.

How long does a project take?

A quotation is issued within 24 hours of receiving a complete brief. Typical project timelines: DFM review or single-part modelling, 3–7 days; full mechanical design, 2–4 weeks; complex product development, 1–3 months. Rush availability on request.

02 · Manufacturing
What is the minimum order quantity?

One. We routinely produce single parts for prototyping. MOQs only apply to processes that require tooling — injection moulding starts at ~50 units, vacuum casting at 1–20 per silicone mould. CNC, laser, 3D print, sheet metal, and assembly have no minimum.

What tolerances can you hold?

Standard CNC machining: ±0.05 mm general, ±0.005 mm on toleranced features. Sheet metal: ±0.2 mm cut, ±0.5° bend. 3D printing: ±0.1 mm (SLA), ±0.3 mm (FDM). Tighter tolerances available on request and quoted accordingly.

What materials do you stock?

Common metals: aluminium 6061/7075, stainless 304/316, mild steel, titanium Grade 5, brass, copper. Plastics: ABS, nylon (PA12), Delrin (POM), PETG, polycarbonate, acrylic. 3D print resins and SLS powders on demand. Custom alloys and specialty materials sourced per project — typically adds 1 week to lead time.

How is a quote calculated?

Every quote is itemised: material cost, machine time, finishing, assembly (if any), QC, and delivery. You see each line — no opaque "estimates." Bulk discounts apply transparently above 25 / 100 / 500 units.

Can I see the parts before they ship?

Yes. Inspection photos and dimensional reports are attached to every order before dispatch. For higher-value orders, we offer in-person inspection at the Doha workshop or third-party inspection via SGS / Bureau Veritas.

03 · Shop & Shipping
Where do you ship?

Doha — same-day courier. GCC (UAE, KSA, Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman) — 3 to 5 working days. International — 7 to 14 working days via DHL / FedEx with full tracking. Bulk and palletised shipments quoted separately.

What's your return policy?

Stocked shop items: 14-day return for any reason, original condition. Custom-manufactured parts are non-returnable unless they fail to meet the agreed specification, in which case we remake at no charge.

How do I pay?

Bank transfer (QAR, USD, EUR), corporate cards, and Wise for international clients. Shop orders are pre-paid. Project work: 50% on engagement, 50% on delivery for engagements under 25,000 QAR; staged milestone billing for larger projects.

04 · General
Are you hiring?

We hire mechanical engineers, industrial designers, and CNC programmers on an ongoing basis. Send a portfolio and short note to studio@gestaltung.qa. Workshop and assembly roles posted separately when open.

Do you take vendor / supplier applications?

Yes — we maintain an active vendor network for specialised processes (anodising, plating, heat treatment, specific tooling). Apply via the Vendor portal or email a capability summary.

Where are you located?

Studio and workshop in Doha, Qatar (Lusail Marina District). Hours Sunday to Thursday, 09:00 to 18:00 AST. Visits by appointment. See Contact for full details and map link.
